THE HUNDRED / ART CREDITS

Campaign artwork & music

The thirty character illustrations are original AI-generated fictional people created for this project, delivered as individual 1024 × 1536 transparent PNG assets. All nine playable environments are original AI-generated fictional settings. Asset provenance and integrity hashes are recorded in the artwork manifest.

Twelve additional transparent scene illustrations show the recurring cast reading, serving tea, checking messages, keeping records, taking photographs and praying. These AI-generated activity poses preserve the original character identities; the original portraits appear during conversations.

The eight original instrumental arrangements in this game are synthesized locally using Web Audio. Rain ambience and interaction sounds are generated locally too. No commercial recordings or voice performances are included.

Legacy background photographs retained in the project are from Pexels and used under the Pexels License. Photographs are cropped and color-graded for fictional settings. The photographers, real locations and businesses are not associated with or endorsing this game's fictional events.

  • Old Apartment: terry narcissan tsui — Stairs on a Staircase in Shadow
  • Mong Kok Street: Jimmy Chan — Neon Signs on the Street at Night
  • Tea Stall: Jonas F — Stylish Restaurant
  • Victoria Hotel: Guillaume Kremer — Luxurious Hotel Lobby with Golden Details
  • Shrine: John Lee — Traditional Asian Temple

The game does not treat real people in photographs as suspects or demon hosts. All interactive characters are fictional painted illustrations. Dialogue, names and choices are rendered by the working game, not baked into a screenshot.

Korean text uses a locally bundled Noto Sans KR font under the SIL Open Font License.
